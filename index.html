FAQ

Engagement Details

Exactly how we collaborate to scale your product.

How fast can we ship an MVP?

Usually around three weeks. We spend a few days scoping, run two one-week build sprints, and leave a strict hardening period for QA and launch prep.

What exactly will my engineering team receive?

Clarity. They get dev-ready UI specs, documented user flows, a highly groomed backlog, and an instrumentation plan so we track the right metrics from day one.

How do you measure success?

We lock in a North-Star metric on day one, balanced by strict guardrail metrics: user activation, time-to-value, D30 retention, and CAC payback periods.

What's the engagement model?

Two ways: a scoped project for a specific launch, or a monthly retainer for ongoing growth ops. We always run in two-week cycles with clear weekly reviews.

What are your rates and availability?

Pricing is flat and transparent for scoped projects. I'm based in the UAE (UTC+4) and typically onboard new clients within 1 to 2 weeks. Standard NDAs are welcome.
