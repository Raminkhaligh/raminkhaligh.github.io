The Bottleneck

Context: You have a strong engineering team shipping updates regularly, but market adoption is slow and sales cycles are stalling.

Problem: Your backlog is driven by internal assumptions or a vocal minority of users, not structural market demand. When product builds features marketing can't position and sales can't sell, you burn expensive development hours.

The Fix

Action: We halt vanity features. I audit your backlog, lock in strict product positioning, and tie every roadmap initiative to a core OKR — acquisition, activation, or retention.

Result: Total cross-functional alignment. You enter the market faster with a go-to-market framework that tells your growth team exactly who to target and how to convert them.
