The Bottleneck

Context: You have a working backend and a marketing engine driving sign-ups, but your Day-1 retention is dropping.

Problem: Most SaaS and fintech products leak revenue because they rely on interfaces that look modern but fail to guide the user. Complex dashboards, heavy cognitive load, and unclear next steps kill user activation.

The Fix

Action: I strip the interface down to the essential user flows. We prioritize search intent and behavioral data to map the shortest path to user value. I deliver strict wireframes and scalable component systems.

Result: Engineering gets clear specs without guesswork. Users experience immediate value. You see a measurable lift in activation rate and a drop in support tickets.
