01. The Problem Space

The Danger of Upstream Misalignment

In the hardware market, selecting the wrong SKUs from HQ or driving demand without controlling the supply channel leads to dead inventory and price collapse.

As Product Marketing and GTM Manager, I was tasked with expanding our mobile market footprint and introducing a new ecosystem product line. Physical retail coverage in major cities was stagnant, and Customer Acquisition Cost was climbing.

The core challenge was structural. Import products with specifications that don't match local purchasing power, or miscalculate "sell-in" (inventory moving to distributors) against "sell-out" (inventory moving to end-users), and the channel floods. Oversupply causes retailers to slash prices, hurting brand perception and margin at once. We needed one strategy spanning global product selection through localized retail execution, not a series of disconnected campaigns.

02. The Strategy

Full Lifecycle Ownership and the 7P Mix

The Engine Behind the Growth

We replaced reactive advertising with total lifecycle ownership, structured around a 7P framework: Product, Price, Place, Promotion, People, Process, and Physical Evidence. Every element had to serve both the B2B supply chain and the B2C consumer.

The catalyst: forecasting demand before launch let us build a Product Bundle (Mobile + Huawei Freelace) backed by channel incentives, lowering effective CAC while introducing the new wearable ecosystem, without cutting the core handset price.

03. The Architecture

Executing the End-to-End Rollout

Capturing 10% market share in a single quarter took synchronized execution across global product selection, supply logistics, and omnichannel marketing.

Phase 1 — Global Portfolio Selection & Forecasting

Before any marketing material was drafted, I evaluated Huawei HQ's global product pool to select the models suited to our region: matching hardware specs to local purchasing power, setting the pricing strategy, and calculating import quantities to avoid dead inventory.

Phase 2 — Supply Chain Mathematics

Once the SKUs were locked, I set up daily KPI tracking for sell-in vs. sell-out. By metering inventory distribution across regions, we avoided both stockouts and the price dumping that plagues oversupplied markets.

Phase 3 — The Nowruz Bundling Strategy

We launched a 360-degree Nowruz Festival campaign anchored by the Mobile + Huawei Freelace bundle: a clear value story for consumers, and for the business, a way to introduce wearable tech while protecting the handset's premium price.

Phase 4 — B2B Expansion & Partner Workshops

I managed partner relationships directly on the ground. Through targeted channel incentives and in-person workshops for regional distributors, we expanded physical mobile shop coverage by 40% across major cities.

Phase 5 — Digital Exclusivity

For digital, we partnered exclusively with Digikala (the region's largest e-commerce platform), concentrating spend into a single, optimized funnel with accurate, trackable ROI.
